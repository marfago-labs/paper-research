# Papers

One row per link. Escape any `|` in a title as `\|`.

- **ID:** `ARXIV:...`, `DOI:...`, or the link for anything else.
- **Status:** `read`, `skim`, `archive`, `done`, `dropped`, or `unread-link`.
- **Rating:** 1–5 after reading, otherwise `-`.
- **Added:** date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
