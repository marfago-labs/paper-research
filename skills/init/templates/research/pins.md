# Pins

One entry per fact, in this format:

`- **[type] Title:** One or two sentences. Source: <ID>, <section, page, or figure>. Status: <status>. Tags: <tags>. Added: YYYY-MM-DD.`

- **Type:** `concept`, `finding`, `method`, `tool`, `dataset`, or `idea`.
- **Status:** `verified` (checked against the source), `from-skim` (not checked), or `user-note` (the user's own idea or statement).
